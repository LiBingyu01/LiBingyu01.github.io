01About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), supervised by Prof. Xuelong Li. My research focuses on multimodal large models for visual perception and embodied intelligence and world models, connecting visual understanding with intelligent action in complex and dynamic environments.

Research Focus

  • Multimodal Large Models for Visual Perception
  • Embodied Intelligence & World (Action) Models
